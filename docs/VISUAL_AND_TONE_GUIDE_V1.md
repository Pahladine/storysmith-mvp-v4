\# StorySmith Visual \& Tone Guide (V1)



\- Palette: warm creams, soft oranges, desaturated blues/teals.

\- Typography: friendly serif for story text, clean sans for UI.

\- /start: whimsical, welcoming.

\- /build: feels like a workshop or story lab.

\- /preview: calm nighttime reading corner.



Screenshots from prior StorySmith versions can be linked here or described in words:

\- "Act I hero intro" – dialogue-style introduction, character portrait frame, etc.

\- "Animated milestones" – not required for v4 MVP, but tone should support future animation.





Exports



\- HTML book: treat as the “deluxe storybook” – warm cream background, generous margins, serif body text, subtle dividers, and a feeling of turning “virtual pages.

\- PDF: treat as “neat printout” – same story content, but simpler layout optimized for paper and home printers.
